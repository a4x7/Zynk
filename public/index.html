Zynk
Short links. Zero bloat.
Zynk turns long, messy URLs into short ones you can actually share. No ads, no analytics dashboards you didn't ask for, no accounts you can't delete. Paste a link, get a short code back, done.
How it works (three-step section):
  • Sign up. Takes ten seconds — username, email, password.
  • Shorten a link. Paste any URL. Optionally set how long it should live.
  • Share it. Your short link redirects to the original — until it expires.
